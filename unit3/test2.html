ĐỀ KIỂM TRA SỐ 2 - UNIT 3: BÀI TẬP NGHE (MY BEST FRIEND)

🎧 Luyện Nghe: Lan's Best Friend (Minh)

Nhấn nút bên dưới để nghe bài giới thiệu của Lan về người bạn thân nhất của cô ấy.

Bài đọc (Audio Script):
"My best friend is Minh. He is twelve years old and he is my classmate at Secondary School. Minh is tall and slim. He has short black hair, a round face, and big brown eyes. Minh is very kind and hard-working. He always helps me with difficult Maths exercises. He is also very clever and creative. He likes drawing comic books in his free time. This Saturday afternoon, Minh and I are going to the town library to read science books together."
