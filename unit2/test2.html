ĐỀ KIỂM TRA SỐ 2 - UNIT 2: BÀI TẬP NGHE (LISTENING SKILL)

🎧 Luyện Nghe: Mi's House in Da Nang

Nhấn vào nút bên dưới để nghe bài nói của bạn Mi giới thiệu về ngôi nhà của mình.

Bài đọc (Audio Script):
"Hi, my name is Mi. I live in a town house in Da Nang with my family. My house is big. It has three floors and six rooms. On the first floor, there is a living room, a kitchen, and a bathroom. The kitchen has a big fridge, a cupboard, and a microwave. On the second floor, there are two bedrooms and a bathroom. My bedroom is next to my parents' bedroom. It has a bed, a wardrobe, a desk, and a chair. There is a poster of my favorite singer on the wall. On the top floor, there is a small attic where we keep old things. I love my house because it is very cozy."
